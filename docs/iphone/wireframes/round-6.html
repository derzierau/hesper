Decided in this round

  • Four own screens (Hello, Scan, Sign in, Connected) plus the language, then the app. No tour; tips in context.
  • The Mac vouches for the phone: a scanned one-time code approves the iPhone at once. The phone proves it knows the secret (an HMAC over its keys) without sending it, so the relay cannot reuse it. Code comparison stays as the fallback.
  • Permissions at their moment: camera on Scan, notifications after Connected (one Continue, then the system alert), microphone on the first hold, Face ID on the first strong-key use. Live Activity needs none.
  • Sample Macs as the demo mode, no permissions asked there.
